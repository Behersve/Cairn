# Standard Deviations of Experimental Results

Standard deviations (SD) are computed over five runs with different random seeds.
For point-wise missingness, MSE and MAE are first averaged over the four test-time missingness rates (0.2, 0.4, 0.6, and 0.8) within each run, and the SD is computed over runs.
ETTh Avg. and ETTm Avg. average the two variants within each run before the SD is computed.
In Table S2, each dataset entry is the SD of the MSE averaged over the four missingness patterns, and Avg. is the SD of the MSE averaged over the six datasets.
